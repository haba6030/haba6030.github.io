About Me

Hi! I'm an undergraduate student at Seoul National University, pursuing triple majors in Linguistics, Psychology, and Computer Science. I am currently working as a Research Assistant under Jiook Cha and Woo-Young Ahn. I also worked as a visiting researcher under Yuan-Wei Yao at the University of Hong Kong.

I am looking for a Ph.D. position in Computational Neuroscience. My research interest is in identifying the principles linking behavior and neural activity by identifying the latent structure they share. I aim to use these principles both to better understand brain and cognition through computational models and to build more human-inspired AI systems.
